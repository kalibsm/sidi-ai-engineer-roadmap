# The Road to AI Engineer

An interactive 3D roadmap of my transition from full stack developer to AI engineer, November 2026 to February 2027.

Walk through a forest village where each house is a level of the plan. Clear a house's quests and its boss challenge to light up the next one.

| House | Phase | Dates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bootcamp Village | Profile fixes | Oct 29 – Nov 1, 2026 |
| Python Plains | Python and AI foundations | Nov 2 – 22, 2026 |
| Embedding Woods | LLM skills: RAG, agents, evals | Nov 23 – Dec 27, 2026 |
| Portfolio Peaks | Three deployed AI projects | Dec 28, 2026 – Jan 31, 2027 |
| Production Foundry | Docker, AWS, observability, cost | Feb 1 – 21, 2027 |
| Hiring Castle | Job search | From Feb 22, 2027 |

## Controls

- **Computer:** WASD or arrow keys to walk, drag to look, `E` to enter a house, click a house to walk to it.
- **Phone:** the round pad walks, swipe to look, tap a house to walk to it.
- Tap any glowing word on the path to read a short explanation of that AI term.

Your progress and where you were standing are saved in your browser, so a return visit opens with **Continue** at the house you are working on.

## Tech

A single `index.html` with [three.js](https://threejs.org/) r128 loaded from cdnjs. No build step.

## Run it

Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder with any static server. To host it, enable GitHub Pages on the `main` branch.
